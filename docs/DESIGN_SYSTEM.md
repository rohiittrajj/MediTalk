# Design system

## Product feel
Calm, trustworthy, practical, human-made, accessible. Patient flows should feel guided, not like dense administrative forms. Clinician views may be information-dense but need clear hierarchy and provenance labels.

## Brand
Name: MediTalk. Logo direction: simple “MT” mark with a stethoscope integrated into or replacing the “T”, with the MediTalk wordmark below when suitable.

The exact OneService palette has not been supplied in this repository. Palette matching is **pending source verification**. Do not claim to match it or guess exact brand colors. Keep tokens centralized and replace starter tokens after inspecting the original assets.

## Rules
- No neon, glow effects, sci-fi patterns, excessive gradients, or generic AI dashboard aesthetics.
- Use restrained surfaces, whitespace, readable typography, consistent spacing, and useful icons.
- Provide clear loading, success, warning, urgent, empty, and error states.
- Do not rely on color alone to convey status.
- Support kiosk, tablet, and desktop; touch targets, keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, screen-reader announcements, scalable text, and readable contrast.
- Provide speech alternatives and touch/text fallback.
- Use plain language for consent and urgent instructions.
