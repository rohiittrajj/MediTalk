About Dashavidha Pariksha (दशविध परीक्षा)

Classical Ayurvedic diagnosis involves the tenfold clinical investigation to determine patient vitality (Bala) and disease severity (Roga Bala). Select the findings corresponding to the patient's constitution and examination. All selections synchronize automatically into the final Medical Summary and Doctor Portal.

1. Prakriti (प्रकृति)
Constitution

Inherent genetic/biological constitution (Dosha balance).

2. Vikriti (विकृति)
Imbalance

Current pathological deviation / disease state.

3. Sara (सार)
Tissue Quality

Excellence and purity of seven vital bodily tissues (Dhatus).

4. Samhanana (संहनन)
Body Compactness

Compactness of musculature, bone joints & symmetry.

5. Pramana (प्रमाण)
Anthropometry

Proportionate bodily measurements, height and BMI.

6. Satmya (सात्म्य)
Adaptability

Habituation to diet, climate, and environmental factors.

7. Satva (सत्त्व)
Mental Fortitude

Psychological stamina, pain threshold, and emotional stability.

8. Ahara Shakti (आहार शक्ति)
Digestive Fire (Agni)

Ingestion capacity (Abhyavaharana) and digestive power (Jarana).

9. Vyayama Shakti (व्यायाम शक्ति)
Physical Endurance

Capacity to perform physical work without fatigue/dyspnea.

10. Vaya (वय)
Life Stage

Chronological age stage influencing Dosha dominance.
