Workflow

How MediTalk works

A structured, end-to-end process that keeps the patient in control and the clinician well-informed.

1
Identify & Consent

Patient identity is confirmed, data use is explained clearly, and explicit consent is obtained for each purpose.

2
Share Your History

Answer adaptive questions by voice or text. Upload existing medical documents. Unknown and declined answers are recorded — not inferred.

3
Draft Is Generated

A structured summary is created with source labels (patient-reported, document-extracted, or system-generated) and uncertainty flags.

4
Clinician Reviews & Accepts

An authorized clinician reviews, edits, and accepts or rejects the draft. The record is finalised only after clinical approval.

About MediTalk

Built for clarity, not complexity

MediTalk is a patient-history intelligence and clinical preparation platform. It helps bridge the gap between what patients want to communicate and what clinicians need to know — through accessible, guided interactions.

The platform supports English and Hindi, works on kiosks, tablets, and desktops, and provides touch and voice alternatives so that no patient is excluded due to literacy or technology barriers.
